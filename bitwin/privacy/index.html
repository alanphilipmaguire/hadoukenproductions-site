Privacy Policy

Effective 5 October 2026 · Hadouken Productions

The short version: Bitwin keeps everything on your device. We don't have accounts, servers, analytics, ads or trackers, so we never receive your habits or anything else you enter in the app.

This policy explains how Bitwin (“the app”), made by Hadouken Productions (“we”, “us”), handles information. It applies to the Bitwin app for iPhone and iPad.

Information stored on your device

To work, Bitwin saves the following only on your device:

WhatWhy
The name you enter during setupTo greet you in the app
Whether you chose the adult or kid-friendly suggestionsTo show the right starter habits
Your habits, chosen emoji, and the dates you completed themTo track streaks, perfect days and crystals
Whether Bitwin Pro is unlocked, and how many icon changes you've usedTo apply the free and Pro features

This information is not sent to us or anyone else. We can't see it, and we can't recover it for you if it's lost.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, this information may be included in those backups. Apple manages those backups under its own privacy policy.

Information we do not collect

  • No account, sign-in, email address or phone number
  • No location, contacts, photos, health data or device identifiers
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we don't collect it

In-app purchases

Bitwin Pro is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your name, Apple ID or payment details. The app only checks with Apple, on your device, whether your Apple ID owns Bitwin Pro. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you, and we don't add you to any mailing list. You can ask us to delete your emails at any time.

Children

Bitwin offers optional kid-friendly habit suggestions. The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children. The name entered during setup stays on the device. Purchases go through Apple, so parents can use Ask to Buy and Screen Time to approve them.

Deleting your information

You're in control. In Bitwin, go to Settings → Reset all data to erase your habits and name, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Your Pro purchase stays with your Apple ID and can be restored at any time.

Your rights

Because we don't hold personal information about app users, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you've emailed us and want a copy of that correspondence or want it deleted, contact us and we'll help. This policy is intended to meet the Australian Privacy Principles and similar laws elsewhere.

Changes to this policy

If Bitwin ever starts collecting information, for example through a new feature, we'll update this policy and its effective date before that change ships.
